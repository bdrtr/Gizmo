The .glb files here are eight of the pack's glTF models (tree_single_A/B, rock_single_A/B/C,
building_home_A_red, building_home_B_blue, building_well_blue), each packed with its .bin and the
pack's shared texture (hexagons_medieval.png) into one binary glTF for embedding in Gizmo's
`dunya` game. Nothing else is changed.
