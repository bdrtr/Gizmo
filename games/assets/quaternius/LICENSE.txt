threejsfundamentals (gfxfundamentals/threejsfundamentals, resources/models/animals/Sheep.gltf),

Deer: the pack's Deer.gltf, its embedded buffer unpacked into a binary glTF, keeping six of its
thirteen clips (Idle, Idle_2, Idle_Headlow, Eating, Walk, Gallop). Geometry, skin, vertex colours
and the kept clips are unchanged.
