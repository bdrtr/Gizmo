The four .glb files here (Knight, Rogue, Mage, Barbarian) are the pack's character files
trimmed for Gizmo's `dunya` game: each keeps only the character's own body parts (weapons and
shields removed) and thirteen of the pack's 76 animation clips (Idle, Unarmed_Idle, Walking_A,
Walking_C, Running_A, Jump_Start, Jump_Idle, Jump_Land, PickUp, Interact, Cheer, Use_Item,
Sit_Floor_Idle), with the binary buffer repacked. Geometry, skin, texture and the kept clips are
unchanged.
