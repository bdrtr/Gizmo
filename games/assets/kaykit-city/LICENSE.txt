car_sedan, car_hatchback, car_taxi: the pack's glTF models, each
packed with its .bin and the pack's shared texture (citybits_texture.png) into one binary glTF for
embedding in Gizmo's `dunya` game. Nothing else is changed; the wheels stay separate nodes, which
the game drives with the engine's `WheelVisual`.
